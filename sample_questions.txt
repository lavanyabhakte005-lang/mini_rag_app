Sample Question 2:
What are the major tourist destinations in India?

Answer:
The major tourist destinations in India include the Taj Mahal in Agra, Jaipur in Rajasthan, and places such as ancient Buddhist sites, historic cities, temples, forts, and monuments.


Sample Question 3:
Why is tourism important for India?

Answer:
Tourism is important for India because it creates employment opportunities, supports local businesses, generates income, promotes handicrafts and traditional industries, encourages preservation of historical monuments and cultural traditions, promotes India's image as a diverse cultural destination, encourages interaction between domestic and international tourists, supports employment, transportation, hospitality, handicrafts, and local businesses, and helps promote India's cultural heritage.
